## 📖 About

🔹 **RimWorld — RimFridge** is a powerful tool built to help you add small, in-room refrigerators and food preservation units to your RimWorld base. RimFridge adds compact coolers that keep food fresh without requiring a walk to a distant refrigerated warehouse.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **RimWorld — RimFridge** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their RimWorld modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Compact Coolers | Small fridge units that fit in bedrooms, kitchens, and hospitals |
| Temperature Control | Adjustable temperature settings for different food types |
| Power Efficient | Lower power draw than full freezer rooms for small food stocks |
| Stackable Storage | Store meals and ingredients in organized fridge compartments |
